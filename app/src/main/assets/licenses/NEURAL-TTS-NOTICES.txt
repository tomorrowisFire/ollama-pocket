OnHand AI embedded neural speech

Runtime: sherpa-onnx 2.0.3 (Apache-2.0), Copyright Xiaomi Corporation and contributors.
Pinned source: https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/commit/38016c6e0622e90bf417c35e8b47e0eb805af417
The app uses the CPU C API. It does not include sherpa's Java/Kotlin classes,
external phonemizers, espeak-ng, piper-phonemize or their voice data.
The removal of those phonemizer dependencies is recorded in the 2.0.0 section of:
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/blob/v2.0.3/CHANGELOG.md
