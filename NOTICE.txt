  https://github.com/RadekParek/Rllama

  https://github.com/ollama/ollama

Their original copyright notices and license terms apply independently.
Do not assume model weights are covered by this app's MIT-0 license: check each model.

Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform are third-party services.
This development build uses Google official sample/demo IDs (no publisher revenue).
Banner requests can transmit device/network data to Google after UMP consent handling.
By contrast, local LLM prompts are sent only to http://127.0.0.1:11434.
Network data is used when downloading models and displaying ads; mobile data fees
may apply under the user's carrier plan.

Before public distribution, complete license review, UMP/privacy configuration,
replace sample AdMob IDs, provide privacy policy and complete device QA.

OPTIONAL VULKAN RUNNER (v0.4):
  https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp (MIT license)
  The Android Vulkan sidecar is a separate llama.cpp HTTP inference server.
  It is not a GPU-enabled Android Ollama binary. CPU fallback is provided.
  NPU hardware detection is not an NPU inference implementation.
